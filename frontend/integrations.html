Align the exports. Normalize dates and provider resource names, convert EUR and USD to CHF at the monthly case rate, deduplicate invoices, keep final contracts and normalize storage units. Identify estimates for missing charges.

Allocate once. Map resource and tool to department. Allocate shared costs by FTE. Use agent runs to explain gateway charges and supplier reports to check billed totals.

Calculate the footprint. Usage × energy per unit × applicable PUE gives kWh. Multiply by the hosting region’s grid intensity for location-based CO₂. The Swiss mid case includes imports. Certificate-based estimates use a separate factor.

Test the package. The case requires at least 40% lower grid CO₂ and 25% lower cost, with up to CHF 16,500 for implementation at CHF 1,100 per person-day.
