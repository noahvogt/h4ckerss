# GreenOps for SMEs — knowledge pack

Background for the sprintd Basel GreenTech case. See [case_info.md](case_info.md) for the challenge and deliverables.

## Basel-Stadt and Basel2037

- **Basel-Stadt voted for net zero by 2037 in November 2022.** The canton's current strategy targets direct greenhouse gas emissions within its territory (Scope 1). In this challenge, purchased cloud, AI and SaaS services are treated as indirect emissions for the SME (Scope 3). A Basel company can influence emissions from a Frankfurt workload through its purchasing and operating decisions. [Cantonal climate strategy](https://www.bs.ch/schwerpunkte/klima/klimaschutz) · [ALTYCA case data, “Why this matters for Basel”](https://github.com/Fa-Bee/sprintd-basel-green-ai-data#why-this-matters-for-basel)
- **Basel2037 helps SMEs turn assessments into projects.** The canton and the local business association founded Basel2037. It offers a free initial consultation with a short report on possible measures. Its 2026 SME programme with FHNW and other partners provides coaching and implementation support for up to ten regional businesses. [Basel2037 background](https://basel2037.ch/) · [Initial consultation](https://basel2037.ch/beratung/) · [SME programme](https://basel2037.ch/kmu-foerderprogramm/)
- **SMEs already have carbon accounting tools.** Basel2037 lists the federal Corporate Footprint Calculator and Intep's Emission Tracker, which it highlights for service businesses. These are useful reference points for the sustainability adviser or finance team receiving the prototype's outputs. [Basel2037, “Tools zur Treibhausgasbilanzierung für KMU”](https://basel2037.ch/wissen/)

## ALTYCA

- **ALTYCA is a Basel data and AI consultancy** with around 40 specialists, according to its website. Its work covers strategy and governance, data platforms, analytics, training and operations. ALTYCA and Basel2037 jointly brought this challenge to sprintd. [ALTYCA About Us](https://altyca.ch/en/about-us) · [ALTYCA services](https://altyca.ch/en/service-offering) · [Case factsheet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OVqDHHwTwU9NZnK8Q2yMmgXpr8sZOr7u/view)
- **May 2026 · Baloise data platform.** Microsoft reported that ALTYCA led the implementation of Baloise's Fabric platform. It combines data from multiple systems and uses versioned transformations to give business units consistent reporting data. [Microsoft project report](https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/2026/05/baloise-modernizes-its-data-platform-with-microsoft-fabric-and-altyca/)
- **Basel-Landschaft · Wastewater operations pilot.** ALTYCA and the cantonal industrial services office (AIB) piloted anomaly detection to help operators decide when to clean a digester inlet pipe. [ALTYCA case study, “The full story”](https://altyca.ch/en/customer-stories/shaping-the-wastewater-treatment-plant-of-the-future)

**Implication for our prototype.** Give IT and finance a way to trace an estimate back to a bill or usage record, compare proposed changes, and see who needs to review a change before it goes ahead.
